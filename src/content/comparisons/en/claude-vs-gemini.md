This is the clearest contrast among the big three, because the two tools differ on almost everything: philosophy, price, feature set, and even who they're for.

**Claude bets on depth. Gemini bets on breadth and price.**

## The trade in one sentence

Claude gives you **higher-quality output over a narrower surface**. Gemini gives you a **much wider surface for a quarter of the price** — with lower quality in exactly the areas Claude specialises in.

If you're buying one tool and your work is writing or code, the quality difference earns its keep. If your usage is general and varied, the price difference is hard to ignore.

## The price gap is not a detail

Claude's cheapest paid tier is **$20**. Gemini's is **$4.99** — and it includes cloud storage. Four times the difference.

Over a year: $240 against $60. For a student or a freelancer, that's a number that actually matters.

But weigh the other side: if Claude saves you an hour of editing a week, it's cheaper in practice.

## The biggest gap: image generation

Claude **generates no images at all**. It reads them, analyses them, and extracts text from them, but it does not produce them.

If your work involves any visual content — posts, decks, covers — that alone may settle the decision. Gemini generates high-quality images and connects to Google's other tools.

## Where Claude is clearly ahead

**Code review.** Give it an existing file and ask for a review: it finds logic errors and edge-case problems at a higher rate, and proposes changes that stay consistent across the whole file.

**Long-form writing.** Its prose reads closer to human. Gemini is linguistically correct but leans report-like.

**Honesty.** Claude is more likely to say "I don't have reliable information on this" than to produce a convincing-sounding answer. That reduces the risk without removing it — verify figures and sources regardless of tool.

**Privacy.** Individual conversations aren't used for training by default.

## Where Gemini is clearly ahead

**Price.** An objective point.

**Integration.** It reads your mail, documents, and files directly. Claude has nothing close.

**Context size.** It takes in very large documents in one pass.

**Breadth.** Images, video, audio, search, and the whole Google stack.

## The verdict

**Writing published under your name, or code?** Claude — the quality gap earns the price gap.
**General, varied use on a budget?** Gemini at $4.99 is hard to argue with.
**Need images?** Gemini, because Claude doesn't give you the option.
**Sensitive data?** Claude, on its default of not training on your conversations.
