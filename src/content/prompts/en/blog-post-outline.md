promptText: |
  Write a detailed outline for a blog post about: [topic].
  Target audience: [e.g. beginners, professionals, business owners].
  I need:
  1. A catchy title (3 options)
  2. A short intro (2-3 sentences) explaining why this topic matters
  3. 4-6 subheadings (H2) covering different aspects of the topic
  4. Under each subheading, 2-3 key bullet points (no full detail yet)
  5. A conclusion summarizing the main takeaway with a call to action
---

Use this prompt whenever you want to start a new article quickly without staring at a blank page. It gives you a ready structure you can expand yourself or ask the tool to flesh out section by section.

## When to use it
- When you have a topic but don't know where to start
- When you want to organize your thoughts before writing
- To save planning time and focus on the actual writing
