promptText: |
  Summarize the following meeting using exactly this format:

  ## Overview
  (3-4 sentences covering the meeting's topic and key decisions)

  ## Decisions Made
  (bullet list)

  ## Action Items
  | Task | Owner | Deadline |

  ## Open Items Needing Follow-up
  (bullet list)

  Meeting transcript/notes:
  [paste text here]
---

Saves you the time of writing meeting minutes by hand, and makes sure no one forgets their task.

## When to use it
- After any important team or client meeting
- To document decisions the team can reference later
- To send a quick follow-up to all participants after the meeting
