  Rewrite the following tasks as professional, achievement-based resume bullet points

  Job title: [title]
  Tasks I do: [describe them in your own words]

Turns dry lines like "responsible for marketing" into bullets that show your real impact and improve your interview chances.

## When to use it
- Updating your resume before applying for a job
- Preparing a LinkedIn profile or personal portfolio
- Prepping talking points for a job interview
