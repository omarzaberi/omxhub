AdCreative.ai generates paid-ad visuals — banners, social ads, display creatives — in every size the platforms require, then scores each one for likely conversion performance.

It targets a specific pain: advertisers need dozens of creative variants to test, designers are slow and expensive, and most marketers can't produce them alone. Speed is the whole proposition.

## What sets it apart

- **Every format at once:** Meta, Google Display, and story sizes generated in a single pass
- **Conversion scoring** based on ad performance data, so you have something to rank variants by
- **Brand extraction:** point it at your site and it pulls colors, fonts, and logo automatically
- **Competitor insights** showing the creative approaches others in your category are running
- **Text generation** for headlines and descriptions alongside the visuals

## How to use it

1. Sign up at `adcreative.ai` and connect your website so it can learn your brand
2. Upload product images — generated visuals are far better with real assets than without
3. Generate a batch and sort by conversion score
4. Edit the top two or three; never publish raw output
5. Split test the variants with a small budget before scaling spend

## Tips for better results

- **Supply your own product photos.** Generic stock imagery is where the templated look comes from
- **Use the score to narrow, then judge yourself.** It ranks; it doesn't decide
- **Fix Arabic text placement manually** — layouts assume English copy lengths and Arabic overflows
- **Generate in batches** to conserve credits rather than one at a time
- **Steal structure from competitor insights,** not the creative itself

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you run paid ads regularly, need many variants for testing, have no in-house designer, or manage several brands and need consistent output fast.

**Not a good fit if** you advertise occasionally (credits go to waste), you need distinctive creative that stands out rather than fits in, or you already run Canva efficiently — the overlap is significant.

## Limits and warnings

**Credits are the real constraint.** Ten a month on Starter disappears fast, and the next tier is $249.

**Output is recognizably templated** unless you edit it. Ads that look like everyone else's ads perform like everyone else's ads.

**The conversion score is a prediction, not a result.** Split test before you scale spend behind it.

## Alternatives

**Canva AI** offers more creative control, a genuine free tier, and enough ad templates for most small businesses. **Adobe Firefly** is stronger if you need original imagery rather than laid-out ad units. **Jasper** covers the copy side that AdCreative treats as secondary.
