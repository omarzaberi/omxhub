Canva AI (known as Magic Studio) is a suite of AI tools built into the popular Canva platform, helping you design, generate images, and edit content quickly without prior design experience.

## How to use it

1. Go to canva.com and create a free account
2. From the sidebar, select "Magic Studio"
3. Choose the tool you need: image generation, background removal, upscaling, etc.
4. Type a description or upload an image depending on the tool, and get results in seconds
5. To unlock more features, click "Try Canva Pro"
