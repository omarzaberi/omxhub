Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic. If ChatGPT is the broadest tool, Claude is **the specialist for text**: deep analysis, long-form writing, and reading large documents.

The difference people notice fastest is tone — its writing reads less mechanical, so the output usually needs fewer edits before publishing, especially at length.

## What sets it apart

- **Handles very large documents:** drop in a full report, contract, or academic paper and it analyzes the whole thing in one conversation
- **Coherent long-form writing:** holds context and voice across thousands of words without drifting or repeating
- **Strong code review:** explains code, catches logic errors, and suggests architectural improvements rather than surface patches
- **Genuine critique:** ask it to poke holes in your plan and it gives real criticism instead of flattery
- **Files and images:** reads PDF, Word, Excel, and images

## How to use it

1. Go to `claude.ai` and create a free account
2. Type your request in the message box
3. Upload files (PDF, Word, Excel, images) via the attachment icon
4. Use Projects to keep files and standing instructions together for ongoing work
5. Click "Upgrade" in settings to move to Pro

## Tips for better results

- **Give full context upfront:** the more background about the project and audience, the sharper the output
- **Ask for a draft, then revise:** rather than waiting for a perfect first answer, tell it exactly what to change
- **Use it as a critic:** "critique this plan honestly and show me its weaknesses" produces genuinely useful answers
- **Set style by example:** paste writing you like and ask it to match the tone

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you write or edit long-form, work with documents and contracts and reports, are a developer who wants serious code review, are a researcher or graduate student, or want text that needs less cleanup.

**Not a good fit if** you want image generation (it doesn't do it), need direct integration with Google services (Gemini fits better), or use AI heavily every day on a tight budget (its free cap is narrower).

## Limits and warnings

**The free cap is tighter than competitors'** — you'll hit it quickly with long documents.

**It can hallucinate like any model.** More accurate than average, but it can still invent a reference or figure confidently. Verify anything important.

**No image or video generation** — text and analysis only.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT** is broader and easier for beginners. **Gemini** is better inside Google's ecosystem. **DeepSeek** is a strong low-cost option for coding. Best approach: run the same task through two of them and compare.
