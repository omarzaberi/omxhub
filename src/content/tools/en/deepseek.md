DeepSeek is an AI model built by a Chinese company that gained attention for offering very strong performance at a very low operating cost, especially for writing and coding tasks.

## How to use it

1. Go to chat.deepseek.com and create a free account
2. Type your question or request in the text box
3. Enable "DeepThink" mode for tasks needing deeper reasoning (like math or complex coding)
4. Developers can use the API directly at a much lower cost than competitors
