DeepSeek is an AI model built by a Chinese company, and it became a talking point for one reason: **strong performance at very low cost**. It demonstrated that results near the top-tier models don't necessarily require enormous budgets, which shook assumptions across the industry.

What that means for you as a user: excellent performance on coding, math, and logic — free, with generous limits.

## What sets it apart

- **Strong at coding and math:** among the best free options in these two areas specifically
- **DeepThink mode:** reasons step by step before answering, noticeably improving accuracy on hard problems
- **Partly open source:** developers can run it locally on their own hardware
- **Very cheap API:** the cost gap with competitors is large, which matters if you're building an app
- **Fast** on ordinary responses

## How to use it

1. Go to `chat.deepseek.com` and create a free account
2. Type your question or request
3. Enable "DeepThink" for tasks needing deeper reasoning (math, complex code, logic)
4. Developers: use the API directly from their site at low cost

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you're a developer wanting a strong free assistant, work on math and logic problems, are building an app where cost matters, or want a model you can run locally.

**Not a good fit if** your work is primarily in Arabic (others are stronger), you handle sensitive or customer data, or you need extras like image generation.

## Limits and warnings

**Privacy is the key issue here.** The company is Chinese and data is stored on its servers, with open questions about its privacy policy and data location. Practical rule: use it for general work and avoid sharing sensitive data, trade secrets, or customer information. This applies to all AI tools, but extra care is warranted here.

**Arabic is noticeably weaker** than ChatGPT and Gemini.

**Content restrictions:** it avoids or gives limited answers on politically sensitive topics.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT** is broader and much stronger in Arabic. **Claude** is better for code review and deep analysis. **Gemini** is also free and tied into Google.
