ElevenLabs is a specialized text-to-speech tool that produces extremely realistic AI voices, and also lets you clone anyone's voice from a short sample. It's widely used for podcasts, video, and audiobooks.

## How to use it

1. Go to elevenlabs.io and create a free account
2. Choose "Text to Speech", type your text, and pick a voice
3. Click "Generate" and download the audio file
4. To clone a specific voice, upload a sample (at least 30 seconds) under "Voice Cloning"
