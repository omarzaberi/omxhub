Gamma turns a topic or an outline into a finished presentation — designed, laid out, and illustrated — in about a minute. No template picking, no dragging text boxes, no fighting with alignment.

It also builds documents and simple web pages from the same engine, which makes it less of a slide tool and more of a "turn this idea into something presentable" tool.

## What sets it apart

- **Design handled for you.** Spacing, hierarchy, and imagery are decided automatically, and the results look intentional
- **Three output types** from one workflow: presentations, documents, and web pages
- **Paste-in generation:** drop in an existing document or outline and it becomes a deck
- **Card-based editing** where content reflows automatically instead of breaking the layout
- **Clean export** to PowerPoint and PDF, so it fits into workflows that require those formats
- **Analytics on shared links,** showing who viewed and how far they got

## How to use it

1. Sign in at `gamma.app` and take the 400 free credits
2. Choose presentation, document, or webpage
3. Either describe the topic or paste an outline you've already written
4. Pick a theme, then let it generate the full draft
5. Edit card by card — rewriting the text is where your time should go
6. Export to PowerPoint or share the live link

## Tips for better results

- **Paste your own outline** rather than giving it a topic. The structure you provide is far better than the structure it invents
- **Rewrite the generated text.** The layout is the value here; the prose is placeholder-grade and reads like it
- **Use fewer cards than it suggests.** Its default decks are long, and cutting a third of the slides usually improves them
- **Save credits by editing manually** — regenerating a card costs credits, while typing over the text costs nothing
- **Export to PowerPoint** if the deck has to survive edits by colleagues who don't use Gamma

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you make presentations often and aren't a designer, need a professional-looking deck under time pressure, want to turn an existing document into slides, or build simple landing pages without code.

**Not a good fit if** you need pixel-exact brand compliance, build highly custom data-heavy decks, or want a tool with a sustainable free tier — the one-time credits run out.

## Limits and warnings

**Free credits never refresh.** 400 credits, once. Plan around that.

**Generated writing is weak.** Treat the text as a placeholder that shows you where content goes, not as a draft you can present.

**Branding appears on all free output,** which rules out client work until you upgrade.

## Alternatives

**Canva AI** is broader — presentations plus social graphics and video — and has a real free tier. **Notion AI** suits you better if the content lives in Notion already. Generating an outline in **ChatGPT** and pasting it into Gamma is the combination that gets the best results from both.
