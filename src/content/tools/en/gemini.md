Gemini is Google's AI assistant. Its main strength isn't raw intelligence — it's that **it's already where you work**: inside Gmail, inside Google Docs, inside your browser, and inside your phone if you're on Android.

If most of your day passes through Google services, that integration saves more time than the gap in model quality costs you.

## What sets it apart

- **Direct Google integration:** reads Gmail, edits Docs, searches Drive, uses Maps
- **Strong visual analysis:** among the best at reading images and video
- **Generously free:** the free tier's limits are wider than most competitors'
- **Fast:** among the quickest to respond on everyday tasks
- **Built into the system:** replaces Google Assistant on Android, works inside Chrome

## How to use it

1. Go to `gemini.google.com` with a normal Google account (free)
2. Type your request, or upload an image or file
3. To connect Gmail or Docs, enable it in settings and grant the permissions
4. On Android, long-press the home button to launch it as your assistant
5. Click "Upgrade" for the Google AI Pro plan

## Practical examples

- "Summarize all emails from the sales team this week"
- Upload a photo of an invoice and get the data extracted and organized
- "Write a polite decline to this email"
- Upload a short video and get its contents summarized
- "Build a table in Google Docs comparing these options"

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you use Gmail, Drive, and Docs daily, own an Android phone, want a free tool with generous limits, or work involves analyzing images and video.

**Not a good fit if** you work outside Google's ecosystem, need very deep analysis of long complex text (Claude is stronger), or aren't comfortable granting access to your mail and files.

## Limits and warnings

**Integration means permissions.** Connecting it to Gmail and Drive grants access to personal data. Read the permissions and understand what you're agreeing to.

**On genuinely complex tasks** it can be less thorough than ChatGPT and Claude.

**It hallucinates like the rest.** Verify anything important against an original source.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT** is broader and stronger on complex work. **Claude** is better for long documents and analysis. **Perplexity** fits better if your main goal is sourced research.
