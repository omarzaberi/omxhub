Gemini is Google's AI assistant, built to integrate seamlessly with everyday Google services like Gmail, Docs, and Maps. It's a strong choice for anyone already living in the Google ecosystem.

## How to use it

1. Go to gemini.google.com with a regular (free) Google account
2. Type your request or upload an image/file for analysis
3. Enable direct integration with Gmail or Docs from settings
4. To upgrade, click "Upgrade" and choose the Google AI Pro plan
