Grammarly is the writing assistant that works where you already write — Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Word, Slack, and nearly every text box in the browser. You don't open it; it's simply there.

It predates the current AI wave by years and adapted to it rather than being replaced by it. The distinction that keeps it useful: chatbots generate text, Grammarly fixes text you're already writing, in the moment you write it.

## What sets it apart

- **Everywhere by default:** browser extension, desktop app, mobile keyboard, Word and Outlook add-ins
- **Real-time correction** as you type, so errors never reach the send button
- **Tone detection** that tells you when a message reads as harsher or more casual than you intended
- **Full-sentence rewrites** on Pro when a sentence is clumsy rather than incorrect
- **Plagiarism checking** against published sources, useful for academic work
- **Snippets and style guides** so a team writes with a consistent voice

## How to use it

1. Install the browser extension or desktop app from `grammarly.com`
2. Write as normal — underlines appear under issues as you type
3. Click a suggestion to accept it, or dismiss it if it's wrong
4. Set your goals (audience, formality, intent) so suggestions match the context
5. Use the rewrite feature on sentences that are correct but awkward

## Tips for better results

- **Set the goals for each document.** A cover letter and a Slack message should be judged differently, and Grammarly only knows if you tell it
- **Ignore it deliberately sometimes.** It pushes toward safe, neutral prose; good writing often breaks its rules on purpose
- **Watch the tone detector on emails** — it catches "this reads as annoyed" before you send something you regret
- **Turn it off on sensitive documents** rather than trusting settings you haven't read
- **Treat the AI detector as a hint,** never as proof

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you write professional English daily, aren't a native speaker and want a safety net, send a lot of email, or are a student who needs plagiarism checking.

**Not a good fit if** you write primarily in Arabic (it offers nothing), need to generate long-form content from scratch, or already run everything through a chatbot before sending.

## Limits and warnings

**English only.** No Arabic, and no sign of it coming.

**Monthly billing is steep** — $30 a month against $12 on the annual plan. If you're going to pay, pay annually.

**It flattens voice.** Accept every suggestion and your writing gets correct and characterless. Push back on the ones that sand off something deliberate.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT** and **Claude** both correct and rewrite text well and handle Arabic, though neither works invisibly inside every app. **Notion AI** covers editing if your writing already lives in Notion.
