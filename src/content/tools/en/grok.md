Grok is the AI assistant built by xAI, and its distinguishing feature is direct, live access to X. While other models rely on a search index that lags behind events, Grok reads posts as they appear.

Its second characteristic is tone. xAI tuned it to answer directly rather than hedge, with noticeably looser content filters than its competitors. Whether that's a feature or a liability depends entirely on what you're asking.

## What sets it apart

- **Live X access:** the fastest model for breaking news, live events, and public sentiment
- **Direct answers** with fewer refusals and less hedging on legitimate questions
- **DeepSearch** for multi-step research across the web on paid tiers
- **Grok Imagine** for image and video generation, included from the Lite tier
- **Extended reasoning modes** for hard problems, available on SuperGrok

## How to use it

1. Go to `grok.com` or open it inside the X app — a free account works immediately
2. Ask about anything current; this is where it beats the alternatives
3. Turn on DeepSearch for research questions that need several sources
4. Use reasoning mode for problems where the first answer is likely to be shallow
5. Upgrade to Lite at $10 if you find yourself hitting the free limit daily

## Tips for better results

- **Use it for "what's happening" questions,** not for careful long-form writing — that's not where it wins
- **Ask it to cite the posts** it's drawing from so you can judge the source yourself
- **Cross-check anything from live social posts.** Trending and true are different things
- **Save your prompts for what matters** on the free tier, given the two-hour window
- **Keep a second model** for polished writing and document work

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you follow news and markets closely, work in media or social, want to track live sentiment on a topic, or find other models' refusals frustrating on legitimate questions.

**Not a good fit if** you need polished long-form writing (Claude is better), you're working with sensitive material where looser filters are a risk, or you want the free tier to carry real daily work.

## Limits and warnings

**The free tier is a demo.** Ten prompts per two hours ends a working session quickly.

**Live access cuts both ways.** It can repeat a viral claim before anyone has verified it.

**Looser filters mean less protection.** It will produce content other models decline — including content you may not want attached to your name.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT** is broader and better for general daily work. **Claude** is stronger for long documents and careful writing. **Perplexity** is the better research tool when you want cited, verifiable sources rather than speed.
