Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant, and its core value isn't that it's the smartest — it's that **it lives inside the applications you already work in**: Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, and Windows.

The practical difference: instead of copying your data into an AI website, Copilot sees the file in front of you and works on it directly.

## What sets it apart

- **Inside Excel:** analyze data and build tables and charts in plain language, without complex formulas
- **Inside Outlook:** summarizes long email threads and drafts replies
- **Inside Teams:** recaps the meeting and extracts tasks and decisions automatically — one of its strongest features
- **Inside Word:** drafts, rewrites, and summarizes documents
- **Built into Windows:** available from the taskbar
- **Enterprise guarantees:** commercial subscribers' data isn't used to train the models

## How to use it

1. Free use: go to `copilot.microsoft.com` with a Microsoft account
2. Inside Office apps: open the app and click the Copilot icon in the ribbon (requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot subscription)
3. Write your request in plain language: "summarize this report" or "chart this data"
4. On Windows: click the Copilot icon in the taskbar

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you work at a company on Microsoft 365, spend your day in Excel and Outlook, have frequent Teams meetings to summarize, or your organization needs compliance and data privacy guarantees.

**Not a good fit if** you're an individual or small business not heavily using Office (the cost is high), want the strongest conversational and analytical performance, or work in Google Workspace (Gemini fits better).

## Limits and warnings

**The cost is high for individuals** — around $30/month per user, aimed primarily at organizations.

**General conversational performance** trails ChatGPT and Claude.

**Results inside Office vary:** excellent at summarizing, sometimes wrong on complex Excel analysis. Always review its output before putting it in a formal report.

**It requires a Microsoft 365 license** before you can add Copilot at all.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT** is stronger conversationally and cheaper for individuals. **Gemini** is the equivalent if your work lives in Google. **Notion AI** if your workspace is Notion.
