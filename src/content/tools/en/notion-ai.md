Notion AI is an assistant built into the popular Notion workspace platform, helping you summarize, write, and organize content without ever leaving your workspace.

## How to use it

1. You need a Notion account first
2. From any page, type `/ai` or click the Notion AI button in the sidebar
3. Choose the action you want: summarize, draft, translate, improve writing
4. For unlimited use, enable the "Notion AI" add-on from billing settings
