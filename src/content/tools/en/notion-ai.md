Notion AI is an AI assistant built into Notion, the notes and project organization platform. Like Copilot with Office, its value isn't the strength of the model but **its location**: it sits inside your workspace and knows what's in it.

Instead of copying your notes into an external tool, you ask it directly about your projects and notes and it answers from your own pages.

## What sets it apart

- **Understands your workspace context:** searches your pages and databases and answers from them
- **Instant summarizing:** condenses a long page or meeting notes in one click
- **Extracts action items:** reads meeting notes and produces a task list
- **Writes in place:** drafts, rewrites, adjusts tone, translates
- **Useful for teams:** when Notion is your team's organizational hub

## How to use it

1. You need a Notion account first (free or paid)
2. From any page, type `/ai` or click the Notion AI button
3. Choose the action: summarize, draft, translate, improve writing
4. To search your workspace, just ask a direct question about your page contents
5. Enable the "Notion AI" add-on in billing settings for expanded use

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you already use Notion daily, work on a team that runs its documentation in Notion, have a large note archive you need to search, or record many meetings that need summarizing.

**Not a good fit if** you don't use Notion (it doesn't work standalone), want a strong general assistant for conversation and analysis, or need image or audio generation.

## Limits and warnings

**Entirely tied to Notion** — meaningless without it.

**An additional cost** on top of your base Notion subscription.

**Weaker on general tasks** outside your document context compared to ChatGPT and Claude.

**Review its output:** summaries can drop important details, especially on long documents or complex meetings.

## Alternatives

**Microsoft Copilot** is the equivalent if your workspace is Office. **ChatGPT** is a stronger general assistant but doesn't know your files. **Claude** is stronger at document analysis if you upload them manually.
