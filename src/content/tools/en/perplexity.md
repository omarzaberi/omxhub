Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine that gives you a direct answer to your question along with the sources it drew from — a strong alternative to traditional search for anyone who wants fast, trustworthy answers.

## How to use it

1. Go to perplexity.ai (usable without an account for a limited number of queries)
2. Type your question in the search box
3. Select "Focus" to choose the type of sources you want (web, academic, Reddit...)
4. Check the cited sources under each answer to verify accuracy
