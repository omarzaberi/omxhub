Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine, and its premise solves the biggest problem with AI tools: **hallucination**. Instead of answering from stored knowledge that may be wrong, it searches the web live and gives you an answer **with a source link for every claim**.

Which means you can verify, rather than believe and hope.

## What sets it apart

- **Sources for every answer:** each statement is numbered and linked to a reference you can open
- **Focus feature:** choose your source type — academic, Reddit, news, or the general web
- **Faster than manual research:** for questions requiring several sources
- **Image and file search:** upload something and search based on it
- **Follow-up questions:** it suggests questions that deepen the research automatically

## How to use it

1. Go to `perplexity.ai` (usable without an account for a limited number of queries)
2. Type your question in the search box
3. Pick a "Focus" to set the source type before searching
4. **Check the cited sources** — don't stop at the answer; open the reference for anything important
5. Use the suggested follow-ups to go deeper

## When it beats the alternatives

- **Research and articles:** when you need information backed by references
- **Product comparisons:** it pulls reviews and prices from multiple sources
- **Recent events:** because it searches live, its information is more current
- **Fact-checking:** the fastest way to verify something you heard

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you're a student or researcher, a journalist or content writer who needs sources, doing research before a purchase, or simply hate opening fifteen tabs.

**Not a good fit if** you want creative writing or long-form drafting (not its strength), need an assistant that works on your files, or want coding help.

## Limits and warnings

**A source existing doesn't make it a good source.** It sometimes cites a random blog or an old article. The responsibility is yours to open it and judge its quality — especially on medical, financial, and legal topics.

**It can summarize a source inaccurately**, so going back to the original matters for anything sensitive.

**It specializes in research, not production.** For a full article in a specific voice, ChatGPT or Claude fit better.

## Alternatives

**ChatGPT with search enabled** does something similar but with less disciplined citation. **Gemini** also searches and ties into Google. But as a dedicated sourced-research tool, Perplexity is the clearest option in the category.
