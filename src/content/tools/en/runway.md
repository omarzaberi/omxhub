Runway is an advanced AI video generation and editing platform, actually used in film and advertising production. You can generate a full video from a text description or turn a still image into motion.

## How to use it

1. Go to runwayml.com and create a free account
2. Choose "Gen-4" or "Text to Video" from the dashboard
3. Type a scene description or upload an image as a starting point
4. Click "Generate" and wait for the video (uses credits based on length and quality)
