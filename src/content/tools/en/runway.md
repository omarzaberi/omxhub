Runway is a platform for generating and editing video with AI, and it's one of the few tools **actually used in real film and advertising production** rather than just experiments.

You can generate a clip from a text description, or — the more practically powerful use — take a still image and turn it into motion.

## What sets it apart

- **Video from text or image** at quality among the best available
- **Animating stills:** upload an image, describe the motion, get a clip
- **Professional editing tools:** background removal, motion tracking, object removal
- **Camera motion control:** specify direction and zoom
- **Real industry use:** not a toy

## How to use it

1. Go to `runwayml.com` and create a free account
2. Pick the generation tool from the dashboard
3. Write a scene description, or upload an image as a starting point (usually better results)
4. Set the motion and clip duration
5. Click "Generate" — credits are consumed based on duration and quality

## Tips that save credits and time

- **Start from an image:** generating from an image gives far more control than text alone
- **Test at low quality first:** lock in the right prompt, then generate at high quality
- **Keep motion descriptions simple:** overly complex ones produce chaotic results
- **Think in short clips:** generate several and assemble them in an editor
- **Expect multiple attempts:** the ideal result rarely comes first try

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you create video content, work in advertising or motion graphics, are a director or producer testing visual ideas quickly, or want to animate images made in other tools.

**Not a good fit if** your budget is very limited (it gets expensive with regular use), you want full long-form videos, or you don't have time for repeated iteration.

## Limits and warnings

**Cost is the main consideration.** The credit system depletes quickly, and video is far more expensive than images or text.

**Clips are short** — you'll need editing to assemble them.

**Consistency is hard:** keeping the same character or location across multiple clips remains a challenge.

**Difficult details:** hands, faces, and text often come out distorted, especially in fast motion.

## Alternatives

Competing video generators are appearing quickly in this space, some cheaper. But Runway remains among the most mature in the professional editing tools that surround the generation itself.
