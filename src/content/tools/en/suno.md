Suno turns a sentence into a finished song. Not a backing loop or a MIDI sketch — a produced track with vocals, lyrics, instrumentation, and a mix, generated in under a minute.

It's the tool that made music generation actually useful rather than a novelty, and the free tier refills every day, so you can test it seriously before deciding whether it's worth paying for.

## What sets it apart

- **Complete songs, not stems:** vocals, instruments, arrangement, and mixing all arrive together
- **Your lyrics, its music:** custom mode accepts your own words with verse and chorus structure tags
- **Genuinely multilingual vocals,** with Arabic among the better-supported languages
- **Daily free credits** — roughly ten songs a day that reset without a subscription
- **Extend and remix:** take a generated track and lengthen it or rework a section instead of starting over

## How to use it

1. Sign up at `suno.com` and take the daily free credits
2. Start in simple mode — describe a genre and mood and see what it produces
3. Switch to custom mode to paste your own lyrics and control the structure
4. Generate two versions of every idea; Suno produces variations and the second is often better
5. Use "extend" to grow a promising 30-second idea into a full track

## Tips for better results

- **Describe production, not emotion:** "warm 70s soul, live drums, analog tape" gives you far more than "happy song"
- **Write your own lyrics.** Its melodies are strong; its lyric writing is generic. This is the single biggest quality upgrade available
- **Use structure tags** like [Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge] so the arrangement has real shape
- **Name a vocal character** — "raspy male baritone", "airy female alto" — rather than leaving the voice to chance
- **Subscribe before generating anything commercial,** because the license attaches at generation time

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you need background music for video or podcasts, want demos of song ideas quickly, make social content that needs original audio, or want to write lyrics without being able to compose or perform.

**Not a good fit if** you're releasing music you want judged as serious craft, need stems for detailed mixing, or work in a genre built on instrumental virtuosity — that's where the seams show most.

## Limits and warnings

**Free-tier output cannot be used commercially,** and paying later does not fix songs already generated. Subscribe first.

**Vocals have artifacts.** Sustained notes and consonant transitions are where trained ears catch it.

**Credits don't roll over** on any tier — daily or monthly, unused allowance simply expires.

## Alternatives

**ElevenLabs** is the better tool when you need spoken voice rather than song. **Canva AI** includes lighter music generation if you're already producing there. For video work, **Kling** pairs naturally with Suno — one supplies the picture, the other the score.
