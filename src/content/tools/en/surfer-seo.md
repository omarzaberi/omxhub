Surfer SEO answers a narrow question very well: what do the pages currently ranking for this keyword actually contain, and what is my draft missing?

It analyzes the top results, then gives you a live checklist as you write — terms to include, headings to cover, target length. SEO stops being intuition and becomes something you can check off.

## What sets it apart

- **Live content editor** that scores your draft against the ranking pages as you type
- **Analysis of real SERPs,** not generic best practices — the guidance changes per keyword
- **Concrete instructions:** which terms to add, how many headings, what length, what subtopics
- **Audit tool** that tells you what to fix on pages you've already published
- **Fits your workflow** through Google Docs and WordPress integrations

## How to use it

1. Subscribe at `surferseo.com` and enter your target keyword
2. Let it analyze the current top results before you write anything
3. Write in the content editor, or paste an existing draft to score it
4. Work the checklist — add missing subtopics first, keywords second
5. Run the audit on older articles that used to rank and have slipped

## Tips for better results

- **Cover missing subtopics before adding keywords.** Topic gaps move rankings; keyword density mostly doesn't
- **Stop around a score of 70–80.** Chasing 95 makes the writing worse and the ranking rarely better
- **Audit your existing pages first.** Fixing an article that ranks tenth is faster and cheaper than writing a new one
- **Verify Arabic keyword volumes elsewhere** before committing to a content plan based on them
- **Ignore the length target when it's absurd** — sometimes the ranking pages are long because they're padded

## Who it's for

**A good fit if** you publish content regularly and need it to rank, run an agency managing client SEO, have a writing team that needs consistent standards, or want to fix pages that underperform.

**Not a good fit if** you publish occasionally (the cost per article is punishing), work mainly in Arabic where the data is thinner, or need keyword research and competitor analysis — that's Semrush's job.

## Limits and warnings

**No free plan.** $79 a month minimum, billed annually. Budget for it or skip it.

**A high score is not a ranking.** Surfer optimizes the on-page factors only. Authority, links, and site health decide far more.

**Arabic data is thin.** Structural guidance still helps; the volume numbers deserve scepticism.

## Alternatives

**Semrush** covers the research and competitor side that Surfer deliberately leaves out. **Jasper** produces the copy but gives you no ranking guidance. A cheap combination that works surprisingly well: use **ChatGPT** to draft and Surfer only to check the draft before publishing.
